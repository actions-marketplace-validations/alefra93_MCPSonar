# Code of conduct

MCPSonar follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- **Be respectful and constructive.** Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume good faith.
- **Be welcoming.** People of every background and experience level are welcome here;
  questions from newcomers are a feature.
- **No harassment.** No insults, personal attacks, sexualized language, or publishing
  anyone's private information. Discriminatory jokes and trolling don't belong here.
- **Security research is welcome, responsibly.** Report vulnerabilities privately (see
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). Never test against MCP servers or MCPSonar instances you don't
  own or aren't authorized to test.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and when
representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through the contact on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/alefra93). Reports are handled confidentially.
Depending on severity, the maintainer may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban a
participant, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
